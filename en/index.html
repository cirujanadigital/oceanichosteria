Puerto López has more to offer than you'd think.

We coordinate excursions right from the lodge. Just ask.

Whale watching in Puerto López

Every year, between June and September, humpback whales arrive from Antarctica to mate and give birth in the warm waters off Puerto López. Watching tours leave from the pier, a few minutes' walk from the lodge, on boats that get close enough to see them breach and slap the water with their tails. For many guests it's the main reason for the trip.

Isla de la Plata – Machalilla National Park

They call it "the poor man's Galápagos" because without traveling 1,000 km offshore you can walk among blue-footed boobies, frigatebirds and albatrosses. The boat crossing takes about an hour and a half from Puerto López and includes snorkeling in clear waters where sea turtles are commonly seen. A full-day excursion, ideal for those wanting something more adventurous than the beach.

Los Frailes Beach in Manabí

White sand, turquoise water and not a single food stall or umbrella in sight: Los Frailes is protected inside Machalilla National Park, so it stays pristine. It's reached by a trail through cliffs and tropical dry forest with an ocean lookout, about 15 minutes by car from the lodge. Worth going early, before the midday tours arrive.

Agua Blanca and Manteña culture

A local community manages this site where the Manteña culture lived more than 700 years ago. The archaeological museum holds original pieces found in the area, and just steps away is a milky-white sulfur lagoon you can swim in; many say the mud is good for the skin. A different kind of morning, part history, part nature, 10 km from the lodge.

Artisanal fishing

Every morning, bright and early, Puerto López fishermen return to shore with the day's catch and sell it straight from their brightly painted boats. It's a tradition going back generations that you can watch by walking along the pier a few steps from the lodge: the fish auction, gulls fighting over scraps, the town waking up. Authentic, with nothing staged for tourists.

Downtown boardwalk

A 10-minute walk from the lodge takes you to the heart of Puerto López: the Fisherman's Monument, handmade craft stalls made of seeds, tagua and shells, and the tourist pier where boats leave for Isla de la Plata. At sunset it fills with people strolling, street vendors, and the best light to watch the sun sink into the Pacific.

Frequently asked questions

Read this before you write to us.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included for all guests, without exception. Hours: 8:00 to 10:00 AM. After that time, there's an extra $5 charge per person.

Is the pool heated?

Yes. Heated during the cold season. Hours: 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM (sunny season) and until 8:00 PM in the cold season.

How do I book?

Via WhatsApp or by calling +593 99 593 0635. A 25% deposit of the total stay is required to confirm. Check-in: 2:00 PM · Check-out: 11:00 AM.

Is it suitable for families with children?

Yes. We have cabins with bunk beds for up to 6 people. All with private access, a garden and a balcony with a hammock. Adults are responsible for minors on the property.

Where exactly are you located?

Malecón Julio Izurieta S/N, north sector, Puerto López, Manabí, Ecuador. A 10-minute walk from downtown, in the quietest area of the boardwalk. We offer free parking.

Do you have WiFi?

Yes. We have WiFi available in common areas and lodging areas so you can stay connected during your stay.

Is there air conditioning in the rooms?

Yes. All of our cabins and rooms have air conditioning to ensure a comfortable stay year-round.

How far is the lodge from Los Frailes Beach?

Los Frailes Beach is about a 15-minute drive from Oceanic Hostería. It's one of the most-visited beaches in Machalilla National Park and a highly recommended excursion for our guests.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería by car?

The access street is C. A-5. If you're coming along the Spondylus Route from the north, turn right at the street next to the gas station. If coming from the south, after passing the Pacha chocolate shop, turn left. If you have any doubt, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in real time.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería from the bus terminal?

From the Puerto López terminal you can take a mototaxi for $1 to the lodge. If arriving by car, the access street is C. A-5: coming along the Spondylus Route from the north, turn right at the street next to the gas station; coming from the south, after passing the Pacha chocolate shop, turn left. If you have any doubt, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in real time.
