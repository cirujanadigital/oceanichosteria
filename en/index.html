From Oceanic Hostería

Puerto López has more to offer than you imagine.

We arrange excursions directly from the lodge. Just ask.

Whale watching in Puerto López

Every year from June to September, humpback whales travel from Antarctica to breed and give birth in the warm waters of Puerto López. Whale-watching tours depart from the dock, just a short walk from the lodge, on boats that get close enough to watch them breach and slap the water with their tails. For many of our guests, it is the reason they made the journey.

Isla de la Plata – Machalilla National Park

Known as "the poor man's Galápagos" — without sailing 1,000 km out to sea, you can walk among blue-footed boobies, frigatebirds and albatrosses. The boat crossing takes about an hour and a half from Puerto López and includes snorkelling in clear waters where sea turtles are a common sight. A full-day excursion, ideal for those looking for something more adventurous than the beach.

Los Frailes Beach, Manabí

White sand, turquoise water and not a single food stand or beach umbrella in sight: Los Frailes is protected within Machalilla National Park and remains pristine. You reach it on foot along a trail through cliffs and dry tropical forest with a sea-view lookout — about 15 minutes by car from the lodge. Worth going early, before the midday tour groups arrive.

Agua Blanca and Manteña culture

A local community manages this site where the Manteña culture lived over 700 years ago. The archaeological museum holds original artefacts found on-site, and a short walk away there is a milky-white sulphurous lagoon where you can swim — many say the mud is good for your skin. A different kind of morning: half history, half nature, just 10 km from the lodge.

Artisanal fishing

Every morning, well before dawn, the fishermen of Puerto López return to shore with the day's catch and sell it straight from their brightly painted boats. It is a ritual that has gone on for generations and one you can witness simply by walking to the dock, steps from the lodge: the fish auction, seagulls fighting for scraps, the town waking up. Authentic — no staging for tourists.

Town centre and seafront

A 10-minute walk from the lodge brings you to the heart of Puerto López: the Fisherman's Monument, stalls selling handmade crafts in seeds, tagua and shells, and the tourist dock where boats depart for Isla de la Plata. At sunset it fills with people strolling, street vendors and the best light of the day to watch the sun sink into the Pacific.

Frequently asked questions

Before you message us, read this.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included for all guests without exception. Hours: 08:00 to 10:00. After that time there is an additional charge of $5 per person.

Is the pool heated?

Yes. Heated during the cool season. Hours: 10:00 to 22:00 (sunny season) and until 20:00 in the cool season.

How do I book?

By WhatsApp or phone at +593 99 593 0635. To confirm, a 25% deposit of the total stay is required. Check-in: 14:00 · Check-out: 11:00.

Is it suitable for families with children?

Yes. We have cabins with bunk beds for up to 6 people. All have private access, a garden and a balcony with hammock. Adults are responsible for minors throughout the property.

Where exactly are you located?

Malecón Julio Izurieta S/N, northern sector, Puerto López, Manabí, Ecuador. A 10-minute walk from the town centre, in the quietest stretch of the seafront. Free parking available on-site.

Do you have WiFi?

Yes. WiFi is available in the common areas and guest accommodation so you can stay connected throughout your stay.

Is there air conditioning in the rooms?

Yes. All our cabins and rooms have air conditioning to ensure a comfortable stay year-round.

How far is Los Frailes Beach from the lodge?

Los Frailes Beach is approximately 15 minutes by car from Oceanic Hostería. It is one of the most visited beaches in Machalilla National Park and a highly recommended excursion for our guests.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería by car?

The access road is C. A-5. Coming from the north along the Ruta del Spondylus, turn right at the street near the petrol station. Coming from the south, turn left just after the Pacha chocolate shop. If in doubt, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in real time.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería from the bus terminal?

From the Puerto López bus terminal you can take a mototaxi for $1 to the lodge. If you are arriving by car, the access road is C. A-5: coming from the north along the Ruta del Spondylus, turn right at the street near the petrol station; coming from the south, turn left just after the Pacha chocolate shop. If in doubt, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in real time.
