Creating Good Memories

True peace and quiet

We're on the quietest stretch of the waterfront, well away from the bars and traffic of the town centre. At Oceanic, evenings pass to the rhythm of the sea: the sound of waves, the sea breeze, and the kind of natural stillness that makes rest actually feel like rest.

Cabins with character

Every space was designed to feel like a retreat: each cabin has its own private entrance, garden, and balcony — the spot where many of our guests end up spending their favourite part of the day. Simple, welcoming, and made for switching off: reading, watching the landscape, or just letting the hours go.

Steps from the Pacific

Just 30 metres from the shoreline, the ocean is part of every moment. It's the first thing you see when you wake up and the last sound before you fall asleep. The beach is accessible directly from the property — perfect for a barefoot walk at sunset.

From Oceanic Hostería

Puerto López has more than you might expect.

We can arrange excursions directly from the lodge. Just ask.

Whale watching in Puerto López

Every year between June and September, humpback whales migrate from Antarctica to the warm waters off Puerto López to breed and nurse their young. Whale-watching tours leave from the pier, just a short walk from the lodge, on boats that get close enough to watch them breach and slap the water with their tails. For many of our guests, this is the main reason for the trip.

Isla de la Plata — Machalilla National Park

It's known as the "poor man's Galápagos" — because without travelling 1,000 km offshore you can walk among blue-footed boobies, frigatebirds, and albatross. The boat crossing from Puerto López takes about an hour and a half and includes snorkelling in clear waters where sea turtles are a common sighting. A full-day excursion, ideal for guests looking for something more adventurous than the beach.

Playa Los Frailes, Manabí

White sand, turquoise water, and not a single food stall or sun umbrella in sight — Los Frailes sits within Machalilla National Park and remains completely unspoilt. You reach it on foot via a trail through clifftops and dry tropical forest with sea lookouts, about 15 minutes by car from the lodge. Worth going early, before the midday tour groups arrive.

Agua Blanca and Manteña culture

A local community manages this site where the Manteña people lived over 700 years ago. The archaeological museum holds original artefacts found in the area, and just steps away there's a milky-white sulphur lagoon you can swim in — many say the mineral mud is wonderful for the skin. A different kind of morning: half history, half nature, 10 km from the lodge.

Artisan fishing

Every morning at the crack of dawn, the fishermen of Puerto López come ashore with the day's catch and sell it straight from their painted wooden boats. It's a ritual that has been going on for generations, and you can witness it simply by walking to the pier steps from the lodge: the fish auction, the gulls fighting over scraps, the town coming to life. Completely authentic, with no tourist staging.

Town centre and waterfront

A 10-minute walk from the lodge brings you to the heart of Puerto López: the Fisherman's Monument, stalls selling handmade crafts in seeds, tagua, and shells, and the tourist pier where boats leave for Isla de la Plata. At sunset the whole promenade fills with people strolling, street vendors, and the best light of the day for watching the sun sink into the Pacific.

Frequently asked questions

Before you message us, read this.

Is breakfast included?

Yes. Breakfast is included for all guests, no exceptions. Served 08:00–10:00. After that time, an additional charge of $5 per person applies.

Is the pool heated?

Yes. Heated during the cold season. Hours: 10:00–22:00 (sunny season) and until 20:00 during the cold season.

How do I make a booking?

By WhatsApp or phone at +593 99 593 0635. A deposit of 25% of the total stay is required to confirm your reservation. Check-in: 14:00 · Check-out: 11:00.

Is the lodge suitable for families with children?

Yes. We have cabins with bunk beds for up to 6 guests, all with private access, garden, and balcony with hammock. Adults are responsible for children throughout the property.

Where exactly are you located?

Malecón Julio Izurieta S/N, north end, Puerto López, Manabí, Ecuador. A 10-minute walk from the town centre, on the quietest stretch of the waterfront. Free parking available on site.

Is there WiFi?

Yes. WiFi is available in the communal areas and throughout the accommodation so you can stay connected during your stay.

Do the rooms have air conditioning?

Yes. All our cabins and rooms are fully air-conditioned to ensure a comfortable stay year-round.

How far is Playa Los Frailes from the lodge?

Playa Los Frailes is approximately 15 minutes by car from Oceanic Hostería. It's one of the most visited beaches in Machalilla National Park and a highly recommended excursion for our guests.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería by car?

The access street is C. A-5. Coming along the Ruta del Spondylus from the north end, turn right at the street near the petrol station. Coming from the south, after passing the Pacha chocolate shop, turn left. If in doubt, message us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you in real time.

How do I get to Oceanic Hostería from the bus station?

From the Puerto López bus terminal you can take a mototaxi for $1 to the lodge. If you're arriving by car, the access street is C. A-5: coming along the Ruta del Spondylus from the north, turn right at the street near the petrol station; coming from the south, turn left after the Pacha chocolate shop. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you with directions in real time.
