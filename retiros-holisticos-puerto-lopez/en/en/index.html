Frequently asked questions

What type of retreats do you host?

Yoga, meditation, wellness, mindfulness, therapeutic training, ancestral medicine, personal development and other group gatherings compatible with the hostería's environment.

What is the maximum capacity?

Up to 60 people sharing beds, 35 people with one bed per guest, or approximately 40 people distributed across rooms and cabins.

Can ceremonies or outdoor activities be held?

Yes. The space has open areas facing the sea and covered sections that allow the program to be adapted according to weather conditions and the group's needs.

Can the food be adapted?

Yes. Vegetarian options are available and specific dietary requirements can be coordinated in advance.

How do participants get there from Quito?

There is direct bus transportation to Puerto López. It can also be accessed from Guayaquil or Manta via ground connection.

Tell us about your retreat.

Let us know your tentative dates, number of participants and the type of activity you'll be running. We'll get back to you with availability and a proposal tailored to your group's needs.
